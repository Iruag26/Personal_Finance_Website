# Personal_Finance_Website
---
## 💰 Personal Finance Assistant

This app helps users understand and manage their money in a way that fits *their* style — from traditional budgeting to modern financial philosophies — with AI assistance.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🧠 Choose Your Money Philosophy
- Let users pick from different financial techniques and philosophies (e.g. 50/30/20, zero-based budgeting, FIRE-inspired, etc.).
- All insights, budgets, and recommendations adapt based on the chosen approach.
- Makes the experience more personalized instead of “one-size-fits-all”.

### 🤖 AI-Powered Financial Planning
- Users can type natural language prompts like:
  - *"Help me plan my savings for the next 6 months."*
  - *"Analyze my spending and show where I’m overspending."*
- AI assists with:
  - Budget suggestions  
  - Expense categorization  
  - Insightful summaries and recommendations  

### 📄 Bank Statement PDF Import
- Upload bank statement PDFs to automatically extract and import transactions.
- Parses dates, descriptions, amounts, and categories (where possible).
- Saves time and reduces manual data entry.

### 📝 Manual Cash Expense Entry
- Add cash expenses that don’t appear in bank statements (e.g. street food, auto, small purchases).
- Keep a complete view of finances by combining:
  - Bank transactions  
  - UPI/card transactions  
  - Cash spending  
- Simple form-based input for quick logging.
